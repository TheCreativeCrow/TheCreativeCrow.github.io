A STRATEGIC TAKE ON ROCK, PAPER, SCISSORS

RoshamboPlus

Every throw uses one of your limited choices. Read your opponent, manage your stacks and decide when to play each move.

HOW TO PLAY

Three familiar moves.
A deeper match.

Each player starts with limited stacks of Rock, Paper and Scissors. Tap or click a stack to play that move for the round.

Rock defeats Scissors  ·  Scissors defeats Paper  ·  Paper defeats Rock

THE STRATEGY

Spend each move wisely.

After a round, the losing piece is removed from its stack. By default, a draw removes a piece from both players. The match continues until one of a player’s Rock, Paper or Scissors stacks reaches zero, so managing your remaining choices matters as much as predicting the next move.

OPPONENTS

Learn how they think.

Opponents have different play styles. Some are predictable; others follow less obvious patterns or none at all. Spotting how they play is part of the challenge.

PREMIUM

More room to plan.

Premium unlocks larger stacks of 7 or 9 pieces for longer, more tactical matches. It also adds Humanlike, Random and Game Aware opponent styles, plus a choice of draw rules: remove both pieces or leave both stacks unchanged.
