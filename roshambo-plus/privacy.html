This policy explains how RoshamboPlus handles information and optional in-app purchases.

+

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Information, advertising and sharing

RoshamboPlus does not ask for personal details or collect or transmit personal information to Creative Crow. The game contains no ads, and Creative Crow does not sell or share personal information from the game.

In-app purchases

RoshamboPlus offers optional in-app purchases to unlock Premium features. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment and Apple Account information under Apple’s privacy policy. The game receives purchase and entitlement information needed to make the purchased features available; Creative Crow does not receive your payment card details through the in-app purchase process. See Apple’s StoreKit transaction documentation.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s privacy practices change, this page will be updated to describe them. The revision date above will also be changed.

Contact

For privacy questions about RoshamboPlus, contact Creative Crow at stonerich.creative@gmail.com.
