TWIZZLE · PRIVACY

Your privacy
matters.

This policy explains how Twizzle handles saved progress, iCloud, Game Center and its optional Premium in-app purchase.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Information, advertising and sharing

Twizzle does not ask for personal details or collect or transmit personal information to Creative Crow. The game contains no ads, and Creative Crow does not sell or share personal information from the game.

Saved games and progress

Twizzle saves your active puzzle, move history, settings and game progress on your device so you can return to your game. This includes level progress, gold, hints, stars and lives.

Private iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, permanent progress syncs automatically through your private iCloud account. This includes level progress, gold, hints, stars, lives and reward and recovery timing. Your active puzzle, move history and audio preferences remain on your device. Offline play remains available when iCloud is unavailable. Apple handles iCloud under Apple’s privacy policy; this sync does not send your progress to Creative Crow.

Game Center

Twizzle uses Apple Game Center for achievements and leaderboards. When you use Game Center, achievement progress and scores for your highest completed level and total gold earned in Quick Games are submitted to Apple. Apple handles Game Center information under Apple’s privacy policy.

Premium in-app purchase

Twizzle offers one optional in-app purchase to unlock Premium features. The purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment and Apple Account information under Apple’s privacy policy. The game receives the purchase and entitlement information needed to make Premium features available. Creative Crow does not receive your payment card details through the in-app purchase process.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s privacy practices change, this page will be updated to describe them. The revision date above will also be changed.

Contact

For privacy questions about Twizzle, contact Creative Crow at stonerich.creative@gmail.com.
